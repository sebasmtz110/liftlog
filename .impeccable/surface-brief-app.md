## Surface

Operate. A phone-sized log used at the gym, also opened on a desktop browser.

## Audience and job

Sebas, between sets. Follow today's split, log the next set, then see whether the weight moved.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The week is seven platforms; today is the one with the safety stripe, and a session is the bar being loaded. This refuses the greeting hero, the dark cinematic workout, and gradient stat tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Painted cinder-block ground, mortar sheets, ink, and one safety-yellow stripe. Barlow, 8px corners, flat fills. The stripe marks today and the set in progress. Real plate colors sit only beside the working weight.

STORY: The day, the lifts, and the last logged weight are visible before the first tap. Start, log, rest, finish. Progress is volume, records, and body weight, in pounds.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navigation names LiftLog (bottom bar on a phone, labeled sidebar when wide). The title is today's split day. Seven bays share one row; today's bay is a full green field capped by the yellow stripe. The exercise list, with scheme and last weight, starts under it, and Start sits in the title row. On a wide screen, recent sessions close the row.

FORM: Painted cinder-block weight room, fifth of the grounded list, seed:9cd8879b7f4822cd. Raises: density from the high-density web (the week and the first lifts share the first screen); field color from the garden map (today is a full color field); arm's-length numerals from the grid specimen (the working weight is large enough to read from the rack and not larger than the title); the queue holds its place from the gate board (the current exercise stays marked through rest); live figure from the specimen (plates and pounds update as the stepper moves).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Constraints

The Library route keeps its exercise list, detail, and movement designs. Sets stay in pounds. Health stays on one account. Missing Supabase config leaves the app open and names the keys to add.
